About Dr. Ananta Kumar Bhakta

Orthopedic & Spine Surgeon

Dr. Ananta Kumar Bhakta is an Orthopedic and Spine Surgeon and Endoscopic Spine Surgeon, serving as Assistant Professor in the Orthopedics Department.

He completed MBBS, joined BCS (Health) and obtained MS in Orthopedics from NITOR. He has completed AO Trauma and Spine Training (India), AO Advance Cadaver Training (India, Hongkong), Fellowship in Endoscopic Spine Surgery and Endoscopic Cadaver Training (India).

Care Philosophy: Accurate diagnosis, safe treatment and clear guidance for every patient — from conservative management to advanced endoscopic spine surgery.
